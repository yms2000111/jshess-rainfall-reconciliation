# Version-matched ECMWF validation: additional protocol v1

Recorded 1 November 2026 in response to review, after retrospective and 2020 outcomes were examined and after some issued 2021–2025 forecasts and observations had been downloaded, but before any version-matched fit or score. This is an added analysis, not a replacement for the 28 October frozen-transfer protocol. Public deposit time will be recorded separately. Historical analyses are not retrospectively preregistered.

## Question and scope
Does aggregate-only climate adjustment add value over rainfall-only coherent benchmarks when calibration hindcasts match the issued forecast's IFS cycle? Retain all eight existing domains and Thursday 00 UTC origins in 2021–2025, control plus perturbed members 1–10, and accumulations ending at hours 168, 336, 504 and 672. The original frozen-model transfer keeps its existing four comparisons and is reported separately.

## Version and date matching
The catalogue production date (`date`) and historical initialisation (`hdate`) are distinct fields. Select production dates within 45 days before or after each target origin, restricted to the same decoded IFS cycle. Use their available historical initialisations, deduplicating exact production-date/hdate/member/lead tuples. Every training target must end before 1 January of the verification year. Do not mix cycles, substitute an adjacent cycle, or silently select another member count. Require at least eight usable production dates and fifteen historical training years; otherwise omit that target from every paired comparison and report the reason. These thresholds remain fixed regardless of scores.

The symmetric production-date window can include reforecasts archived after the target origin. This arm is therefore retrospective cycle-matched calibration, not a reconstruction of information available on that issue date. An availability-constrained sensitivity uses production dates no later than the origin, the same 45-day backward boundary and the same minimum coverage. A production date alone does not prove actual historical release time; preserve that caveat. No look-ahead in verifying rainfall is allowed in either arm.

ECMWF's model table lists Monday/Thursday reforecasts through 48r1. Cycle 49r1, implemented in November 2024, uses odd-numbered days of each month, excluding 29 February. The S2S news page identifies first new-cycle runs as 13 November, whereas the summary table lists 12 November. Resolve boundaries with decoded metadata and catalogue records, retaining both administrative and first-archived dates. Do not assume every Thursday has a same-date reforecast after this change. For 29 February 2024, inspect the documented archive exception rather than inventing historical February 29 initialisations. Catalogue availability must be checked before submitting requests; unexpected dates or fields stop processing.

## Observations, predictors and fitting
Use a fixed AGCD v1.0.4 snapshot for both historical fitting and 2021–2025 verification in this new arm, retaining acquisition dates and hashes. The earlier v1.0.1 archive does not extend through 2025 and must not be described as unchanged. Late-December 2025 targets use the already documented BoM January 2026 bridge; repeat results excluding the four December 4/11/18/25 origins. Preserve regional weighting, units, cumulative windows and documented QC, and report all exclusions.

Retain lagged RMM1/RMM2, Niño3.4, DMI and SAM definitions. Verify date coverage, source snapshots and lag availability before fitting. Do not impute missing predictors from future data; omit affected cases consistently. Revised historical indices make this a retrospective availability approximation. Each fitting or validation split estimates its own scaling, rainfall marginals, climate coefficients, covariance and reordering templates. Exclude historical validation years and neighbours and all target windows crossing their boundaries. Covariance uses held-out residuals within the applicable training set.

Fit fixed H0, H1 at penalty 0.05, BU-rain, BU-clim and raw, plus an additional H1 arm whose penalty is selected from {0.05, 0.50} by mean inner held-out coherent 28-day CRPS, averaged equally over historical years. Ties favour 0.05. Inner validation excludes its targets from all fitted components, with further training-only residual splits for covariance. Refit the selected setting on all permitted historical data and score the issued origin once. The candidate set cannot change after new-period scores.

## Estimation and decision rules
Primary fixed-H1 comparisons are H1 minus BU-rain and H1 minus H0, with Holm adjustment over those two. BU-clim and raw comparisons are secondary estimates. Selected-H1 comparisons against H0 and BU-rain form a separately labelled secondary Holm family; do not promote them after observing results. All configurations use the same eligible cases. Normalise differences by each target's permitted historical raw CRPS, average origins within domain-year, then domains equally, then years equally. Publish the unnormalised millimetre contrasts too.

Report joint-year bootstrap intervals, every domain-year effect, the number of favourable years, and IFS-cycle-stratified descriptive effects with counts. Five years are not assumed to be independent simply because they are separate calendar years: climate persistence and overlapping training sets may induce dependence. Exact two-sided sign flips require symmetric independent annual differences and have minimum attainable p = 0.0625 with five nonzero blocks. Retain this test and do not switch to a one-sided test to obtain significance. Intervals spanning zero mean unresolved direction at this precision. Report success or failure against the stated criterion without changing it: a directional claim requires a negative estimate and Holm-adjusted two-sided p below 0.05; this criterion is unattainable by that exact five-block test. Consequently estimation and consistency, rather than a significance claim, are the intended contribution.

The accompanying retrospective precision file uses the actual twenty-year annual effects. Its 2.8*s/sqrt(5) calculation is only a normal-approximation planning indicator, without multiplicity or small-sample corrections; it is not the attainable detection threshold of the exact sign-flip test and does not establish five-year power. No minimum effect has been selected from the new outcomes.

## Archive and execution
Keep credentials private and do not change Windows security settings. Continue the existing serial issued-forecast worker; do not launch concurrent requests. After that queue is idle, test the reforecast request schema with an availability-only catalogue inspection and one small paired sample, then serially retrieve deduplicated batches, recording request IDs, hashes and audits. Implementation changes discovered before scoring are logged with timestamps and reasons. Publish the protocol and implementation hashes on GitHub; skip Zenodo as requested. Do not upload raw ECMWF or BoM archives in this project package, as requested by the author; provide retrieval scripts and checksums. This is a packaging choice: the current ECDS licence grants ECMWF CC BY 4.0 rights and BoM CC BY-NC 4.0 rights, so a blanket assertion that all S2S data forbid commercial use or redistribution would be inaccurate.
